Visa Master07

02 — Product

What Visa Master Is

Visa Master turns verified visa requirements into a guided workspace on the applicant's own machine — one they can actually execute.

Sourced

Requirements are gathered from official consulate and visa-center sites, then shown as a short checklist — each item carrying its source link and the date it was last checked.

Local

Passport, financial, employment, and family answers are completed and stored in a local workspace, not sent to a general-purpose AI system.

Executable

Official forms arrive pre-downloaded, reference templates fill the gaps, progress is tracked per applicant, and the result exports as an organized package.

First customer: Chinese ordinary-passport holders preparing Schengen and U.S. B1/B2 short-stay visas independently. $29 per application workspace, with family/group and expert-review options added after validation.

Visa Master08

02 — Product

Core Workflow

Six steps from a trip description to a submission-ready package. Personal data is only requested once the structure around it exists.

1

Trip and applicant intake

Nationality, residence, destination, purpose, dates, and group members drive route selection and eligibility-oriented intake.

2

Sourced requirements

Official-source retrieval, citation, timestamp, and jurisdiction mapping produce a concise checklist.

3

Workspace assembly

Form download, file structure, progress states, and dependency tracking are ready before personal data entry.

4

Local completion

A local web server, local storage, encryption, and permission boundaries keep sensitive answers on the device.

5

Guidance

Field-level help and clearly labeled nonofficial reference templates appear next to the work itself.

6

Readiness review

Completeness checks, source links, document naming, and review handoff produce the final package.

Visa Master09

02 — Product

Privacy & Trust Design

Local-first handling is built into the workflow as product behavior, not stated as a policy.

Local by default

Personal answers and uploaded documents remain in the user-controlled local workspace.

Data minimization

Only the non-sensitive context needed for research or guidance reaches an external AI service.

Source transparency

Every requirement links to an official source and shows when it was last checked.

Material labeling

Official forms, guidance, product interpretation, and AI templates are visually distinct.

User control

Users can inspect, export, or delete the workspace at any time.

No approval is promised, and none is implied.

Visa decisions stay with governments. Ambiguous or high-risk guidance is escalated to manual review rather than presented with false certainty — the product competes on accuracy and control, not on outcome claims.

Visa Master10

02 — Product

Material Labeling

Four kinds of material appear in a workspace. The applicant can always tell which is which — that distinction is what makes AI assistance safe in a high-stakes filing.

Official forms

Downloaded directly from the consulate or visa center, unmodified, with the retrieval date recorded.

Official guidance

Quoted requirement text linked back to the page it came from, with a last-checked timestamp.

Product interpretation

Visa Master's plain-language reading of a rule, marked as interpretation rather than as the rule itself.

Reference templates

AI-generated starting points for travel plans and employment letters where no official template exists — labeled nonofficial.

Where a requirement is ambiguous or the confidence is low, the workspace flags it for manual review instead of presenting a guess.

05 — Execution

Risks & Controls

Maintaining trustworthy, current, jurisdiction-specific workflows is the primary challenge.

Risk Why it matters Mitigation
Requirements change by jurisdiction Outdated or generalized instructions cause delays and destroy trust Version every source with last-checked dates; separate shared from consulate-specific rules
Guidance read as legal advice Visa decisions rest with governments; misrepresentation has serious consequences Explicit disclaimers, source-level evidence, confidence flags, no approval guarantees
Sensitive data leakage The product handles passport, financial, employment, and family data Local-first storage, encryption, data minimization, deletion controls, security review
Scope expansion reduces quality Too many destinations too early creates shallow, unreliable workflows Validate Spain Schengen and U.S. B1/B2 first; require route-specific acceptance tests
Established platforms copy features Atlys and iVisa already offer guided, broad online visa services Differentiate on China-specific experience, local-first privacy, executable workspaces, partner distribution
Low willingness to pay Users may prefer free chatbots despite poorer workflow quality Test $29 early; measure time saved, completion, referral, and conversion before scaling spend
Visa Master26

05 — Execution

Operating Approach

Four rules that decide what the team builds next.

Route depth before geographic breadth

Each supported route requires official-source mapping, test cases, and versioned updates before it ships. A shallow route is worse than no route.

Human-in-the-loop quality

Ambiguous or high-risk guidance is escalated to manual review rather than presented with false certainty.

Privacy as product behavior

Local-first handling is built into the workflow itself, not stated only as a policy the user has to trust.

Evidence-driven expansion

Future travel features unlock only after the visa-preparation wedge shows paid demand and repeat engagement.
